Commissioning

    Expert bypass (unlock without commissioning)

    This is the only bypass. The motors then run on the profile's guesses and whatever numbers are already stored; only the top counts as clear for turning the head until a safe-spin height is set.

    Motor & calibration

    Lifter calibration

    Measures travel, the lowest power that moves the lifter each way, and a speed table (several minutes on a 19:1). It homes the head first and moves the mast end to end many times; keep the E-STOP in reach.

    The rotary's ticks per turn come from the rotary test on the Diagnostics page.

    Serial (Marcduino bus)

    WiFi

    WiFi changes take effect after a restart. The access point (the board's own network) is always there; if the home network does not work you can still reach the page on the access point at 192.168.4.1.

    Mesh (WCB)

    Off unless you turn it on. With the mesh on, the periscope joins your WCBs' ESP-NOW network as its own board: it takes :P moves and E-STOPs from it and sends &PERI heartbeats and positions. The mesh then owns the WiFi radio, so the board leaves your home network: after the restart this page is only at http://192.168.4.1 on the board's own network (R2Uppity-…, same password). Turn the mesh off here or with #PMESH0 to go back.

    Copy the values from a WCB's ?BACKUP listing (its serial console or the WCB config tool): the ?MAC,2 and ?MAC,3 lines for the octets, ?EPASS for the password, ?WCBQ for the quantity, ?WCBCH for the channel. Do not type those commands on a WCB to look a value up: they change it. Type the mesh password here only: it is never shown again or printed in the log.

    Firmware

    Update

    Pick the app image, UppityFirmware.ino.bin. The board refuses an update while a motor is enabled or a job is running; nothing can move during the upload. Releases: github.com/highfalutintodd/R2UppitySpinner_ALT/releases

    Restart and reset

    Factory reset erases every v4 setting: motor profile, inversion, travel, safe-spin height, rotary numbers, tuning, the home WiFi network and the access point password, plus the calibration and any stored sequences. The board then restarts LOCKED on its own network (R2Uppity-…, password Astromech). v3's stored values are not touched and are imported again at the next boot.

    Save your settings first: Diagnostics → Configuration → Get the settings text.

    Theme

    Paper is the default. The choice is stored on the board too, so a new phone starts with it.

    Live sensors

    Rotary test

    Raises the mast to the top first if needed, then turns the head and measures ticks per turn from the home switch. Stores the result when the turns agree. Rotary moves are refused below the safe-spin height; the raise takes care of that.

    Paste lines saved earlier. Only #P setting and sequence lines are sent, one at a time; each is saved on the board as it lands.

    Now

    Safety override

    For 60 seconds the mast may come down with the head not home, and the head may be jogged below the safe-spin height. Nothing else is relaxed: with the height unknown only Up works, and the E-STOP ends the override at once.

    Lifter jog

    Hold to move, let go to stop (it also stops by itself 2 s after the last renewal if the WiFi drops). Continuous drive, capped to the profile. Down stops at the safe-spin height unless the head is home or the override is on.

    Power

    Rotary jog

    Hold to turn. Above the safe-spin height this works like a slow spin; below it only with the override on (the head is inside the dome there).

    Power

    Head and faults

    "Set home here" tells the board that the head is lined up with the dome opening right now. Use it only when you can see that it is; the next descent trusts it.
